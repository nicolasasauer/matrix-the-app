## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### No Data Collection

**Matrix – Das Kartentrinkspiel** does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. The Application works entirely offline on Your Device.

* No account registration is required.
* No personal information is requested or stored externally.
* No usage data or analytics are collected.
* No network connections are made by the Application.

### Locally Stored Data
The Application may save user preferences (such as animation settings and player names entered during a game session) exclusively on Your Device using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves Your Device and is not accessible by Us.

You can delete this locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the Application or clearing the app data in Your Device settings.
